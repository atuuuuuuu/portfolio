## Product Principles

1. Show, don't claim: every statement points to something real (code, a release, an article).
2. Emailing him is the visitor's next step and must never be hard to find.
3. Short and scannable for a busy reader; depth is one click away on GitHub.
4. Respect the visitor: no trackers, works with keyboard, touch and reduced motion, prints as a CV.
